Call for Participation

We welcome high-quality submissions on privacy, security, safety, and ethics of AI in pervasive environments.

Submission Types

  • Long Papers: up to 6 pages (excluding references)
  • Short Papers: up to 4 pages (excluding references)
  • Position Papers: follow the same page limits as long/short papers (6 or 4 pages, excluding references)
  • Review: double-blind review by workshop committee/organizers

Submission Notes

  • Submission Deadline: 6.15 6.22
  • Notification of Results: expected 7.15
  • Camera-Ready Deadline: expected 8.4
  • Submit via PCS (Precision Conference System)
  • Submission venue path in PCS: SIGCHI → UbiComp/ISWC Workshop 2026 → UbiComp/ISWC Workshop 2026 PSSEAPE
  • Please format submissions using the ACM sigconf template (two-column, double-blind review): ACM Conference Proceedings Master Template (Overleaf).
  • All submissions must be anonymized
  • Position papers are welcome. Please include "Position: XXX" in the title (a one-sentence statement of your position) to help reviewers better assess your contribution.
  • Accepted papers will appear in adjunct proceedings
